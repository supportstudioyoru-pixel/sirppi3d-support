Privacy Policy

Sirppi3D — Decimation app for Mac - make heavy 3D data light

日本語
Effective: October 2026  |  Operated by Studio_Yoru

Sirppi3D collects no data. Files you open are processed only on your Mac and are never sent anywhere.

1. Information we collect

Sirppi3D does not collect any personal information about you.

  • 3D files you open are processed only on your Mac.
  • Exported files are saved only where you choose (by default, next to the original file).
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

2. Data transmission

Sirppi3D makes no internet connections.

  • The data in files you open is never sent externally.
  • No usage data, crash reports, or diagnostics are sent to any server.

3. Tracking, advertising and analytics

Sirppi3D uses none of the following:

  • Advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • Analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • Tracking technologies (including collection or sharing of device identifiers)

4. Price

Sirppi3D is free and has no in-app purchases. We do not handle any payment information.

5. Storage on your Mac

The app stores the following settings on your Mac (UserDefaults). They never leave your Mac.

  • Language, panel position, display mode, and the Z-up setting
  • Output format, source unit, file name suffix, and advanced settings

The app does not store the contents of files you open or a history of file locations.

6. Children's privacy

Sirppi3D is not directed at children under 13. Because no personal information is collected, the same privacy protection applies regardless of age.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page.

8. Contact

For questions about privacy, please email the address below. Putting "Sirppi3D" in the subject line helps us respond quickly.
